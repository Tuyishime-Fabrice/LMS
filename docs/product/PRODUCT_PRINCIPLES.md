# Product principles

1. **Next action first.** Every learner screen answers "what do I do now?" (Home leads with
   *Needs your attention*, then *Continue learning*).
2. **Truthful status.** Show only states the database confirms; say "awaiting confirmation"
   instead of guessing.
3. **One course, many routes.** Tracks, cohorts and sponsorship reference courses; they never copy them.
4. **Feedback is part of the mark.** No number without words.
5. **Privacy by default.** Collect the minimum, explain why, opt-in for research, let people delete.
6. **Calm, precise interface.** Neutral canvas, white panels, hairlines, green only for action
   and progress, status always with text + icon. No marketing heroes inside the app.
7. **Honest gaps.** A feature that is not wired shows an explanation, never a dead button.
8. **Bilingual by construction.** English and French ship together.
