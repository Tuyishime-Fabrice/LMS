# Product vision

**DATA+ Academy helps people in Rwanda and Francophone Africa build practical data, AI and
analysis skills, alone or with a cohort, and prove them with work an employer can verify.**

## What the LMS is
The learning journey at dataplusacademy.com: discover → onboard → apply/enrol → pay/get
access → learn → assessments, assignments, capstone → feedback → certificate, plus skill
and career tracks and competitions.

## What it is not
- Not the company website (dataplus.com owns about, team, contact, partners, campaigns).
- Not a chat product (Discord hosts cohort discussion).
- Not a code-execution platform (practice runs in Colab/JupyterLite, linked out).
- Not a job-placement service (career tracks prepare; they do not promise employment).

## Hybrid model
One course, two delivery modes. **Self-paced**: access after payment, own speed.
**Instructor-led cohort**: same content plus schedule, live sessions, deadlines, instructor
feedback and a Discord channel. Price, schedule and support differ by cohort; content does
not. Organisations sponsor places or dedicated cohorts rather than receiving copied courses.

## Success looks like
A learner always knows the next action; every status shown is true; every mark has
feedback; instructors see what needs review; admins plan from consented, aggregate data.
